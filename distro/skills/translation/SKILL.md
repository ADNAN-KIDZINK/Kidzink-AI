# Translation

## Approach
- Confirm the target language and audience if it isn't clear (formal letter, social post, internal note).
- Translate meaning, not word by word. Keep the tone and level of formality of the original.
- Keep names, product names, figures, dates and units exactly as they are, unless a local format is clearly wanted.
- Keep the structure: headings, lists and tables stay in the same order.

## Arabic
- Use Modern Standard Arabic for business writing unless asked for a dialect.
- For documents, make a Word file with the word-documents skill; tell the person to check right-to-left layout in Word (Home → Paragraph → Right-to-left) because the script lays out text left-to-right.

## Quality
- Flag anything ambiguous, culturally sensitive or hard to translate, with the choice you made.
- For important documents (contracts, official letters, published material), recommend a check by a fluent speaker.
- Don't translate content that's already in the target language; point it out.
