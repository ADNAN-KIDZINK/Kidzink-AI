# Research

## Searching
- Search: `uvx ddgs text -q "search terms" -m 8` (news: `uvx ddgs news -q "..." -m 8`).
- Read a page: `uvx ddgs extract -u "https://..." -f text_plain`.
- Run several searches with different wording. Open and read the most relevant 3–6 sources; don't rely on search snippets alone.
- Prefer primary sources: official sites, regulators, standards bodies, the supplier's own pages, reputable news. Be wary of marketing pages and undated blog posts.
- For UAE topics, check UAE or Dubai government and regulator sources first.

## Reporting
- Lead with the answer in two or three sentences, then the details.
- Cite every factual claim with its source as a link, and the date when it matters ("as of March 2026").
- Separate facts from opinion or marketing claims. If sources disagree, say so.
- Say what you couldn't find or verify. Never fill gaps from memory as if they were search results; if you add general knowledge, label it.
- Prices and availability change: say they should be confirmed with the supplier.

## When search fails
- If the search commands don't work (no internet, blocked), say so and offer what you know, clearly labelled as possibly out of date.
