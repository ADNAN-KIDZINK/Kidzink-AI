# Meeting preparation

## What to produce (pick what fits)
- **Agenda**: purpose, topics with time and owner, decisions needed. Keep to what fits in the time.
- **Briefing note**: who's attending and what they care about, background in five bullets, Kidzink's goal for the meeting, the key message, numbers to have at hand.
- **Talking points**: the three or four points to land, each with a supporting fact from the person's material.
- **Likely questions**: the tough questions they may get, each with a short suggested answer. Mark answers that need facts the person must supply as [to confirm].
- **Follow-up**: after the meeting, use the summarise skill for notes and the emails-and-messages skill for the follow-up email.

## Rules
- Ask what the meeting is, who it's with and what a good outcome looks like if you don't know.
- Use only the person's facts and figures. Use the research skill if they want background on a company or topic, with sources.
- For slides, agree the storyline first and then use the powerpoint-slides skill.
